People carry heritage forward

Communities & traditions

The Central District is home to communities with distinct histories, languages, customs and family traditions. Learn with curiosity and avoid assuming every person practises the same traditions.

Craft & knowledge

Pottery and handmade objects

Clay vessels and other handmade objects carry practical knowledge about materials, design, food storage and everyday life. Learning from experienced makers can help preserve techniques and the stories connected to them.

Everyday heritage

Objects with a story

Traditional objects are more than decorations. Ask community members what an item is called, how it was made, how it was used and whether its meaning has changed over time.

Leadership & history

Kgosi Khama III and Serowe

Khama III was a major Bangwato leader during a period of significant change. Serowe later became a central home of Bangwato political and cultural life. Oral accounts, archives and historic sites help tell this story from different perspectives.

Performance & identity

Music, dance and storytelling

Dance, praise poetry, folktales and songs can teach language, values, history and community identity. Record performances only with permission and credit the people who share their knowledge.

Respectful research: Ask permission before recording people, ceremonies or culturally sensitive knowledge. Let participants decide what can be published and how they wish to be credited.
