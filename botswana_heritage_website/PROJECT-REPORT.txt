NGWAO BOTSWANA — INTEGRATED PROJECT WEBSITE
===========================================

PROJECT OVERVIEW
Ngwao Botswana is an educational website concept designed to showcase cultural, historical and natural heritage in Botswana's Central District, with special attention to the Tswapong area and Serowe. It also introduces selected heritage places elsewhere in Botswana.

PURPOSE
To present information about local heritage in an engaging, accessible format and encourage young people to learn about and respect the places, traditions and community knowledge that contribute to Botswana's identity.

MAIN CONTENT
1. Old Palapye (Phalatswe): historic settlement and surviving ruins connected with Bangwato history and Kgosi Khama III.
2. Lecheng Pan: local landscape feature. The current visual is a broader regional satellite image, not a verified close-up of the pan; local verification and a specific photograph are recommended.
3. Moremi Gorge: Tswapong landscape with waterfalls and cultural/spiritual importance to local communities.
4. Khama Rhino Sanctuary: community-based wildlife conservation near Serowe.
5. People and traditions: pottery, oral history, cultural performance and traditional leadership.
6. Across Botswana: examples of wider cultural and natural heritage.

DESIGN CHOICES
- Forest green and earthy brown/clay colours communicate landscape, land and craft.
- Cream backgrounds and restrained gold accents support readability.
- A custom static SVG logo uses layered hills, a sun and a clay pot motif. It is not animated and is not a cartoon character.
- Navigation is consistent across all pages and adapts to smaller screens.
- Photographic images and descriptive alternative text support visual learning.
- A Sources page credits information and photographs.

TECHNOLOGY
HTML5, CSS3 and a small JavaScript file. No build tools or installation are required. Open index.html in a browser. Google Fonts are requested online; system fonts remain available if offline.

IMAGE STATUS AND LIMITATION
Photographic images are currently loaded from Wikimedia Commons by URL and are not yet stored as local image files in this ZIP. They therefore require internet access and may not appear offline. The static logo and website code are stored locally. Before final submission, download the relevant image files where permitted, save them in assets/images, update image paths to local files, and check each attribution/licence. The Lecheng Pan image is regional satellite context only and should not be labelled as a direct photograph of the pan.
